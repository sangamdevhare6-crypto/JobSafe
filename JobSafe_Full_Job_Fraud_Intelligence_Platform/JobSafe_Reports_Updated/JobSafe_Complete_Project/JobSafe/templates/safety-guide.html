Job Safety Guide

Simple checks to avoid common recruitment scams.

🚫 Never Pay to Get a Job

Registration, security or training fees are major warning signs.

🌐 Verify the Official Website

Find the role on the company's real careers page.

✉ Check Recruiter Email

Be careful with personal email addresses claiming to represent companies.

🔐 Protect Credentials

Never share OTPs, passwords or banking credentials.

⏱ Ignore Pressure

Urgency and “limited seats” tactics should trigger extra verification.

📸 Save Evidence

Keep screenshots, URLs and messages before reporting a scam.
